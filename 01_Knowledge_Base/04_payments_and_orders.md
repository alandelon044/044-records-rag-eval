# Payments and Orders

## 1. Payment methods

1.1. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay (Google Wallet), and Afterpay.

1.2. We do not accept cash, checks, or payment on delivery.

## 2. Afterpay

2.1. Afterpay lets you split a store order into 4 interest-free payments, due every 2 weeks. The first payment is made at checkout.

2.2. Afterpay approval, payment schedule, and late fees are managed by Afterpay, not by 044 Records.

2.3. Afterpay is available for store orders (merchandise and vinyl), including pre-orders. It is not available for event tickets.

## 3. When you are charged

3.1. Payment is taken at checkout, including for pre-orders. With Afterpay, the first of 4 payments is made at checkout.

3.2. Prices exclude sales tax, which is calculated at checkout.

## 4. Order confirmation

4.1. After checkout, the customer receives an order confirmation email with the order number and receipt.

4.2. If the confirmation email has not arrived within 1 hour, check the spam folder, then contact support.

## 5. Changing or canceling an order

5.1. A store order (merchandise or vinyl) can be changed or canceled within 2 hours after it is placed. Email support@044records.example with your order number.

5.2. The 2 hours are counted until the customer sends the email, not until support replies. A request sent within 2 hours is honored even if support handles it later, for example on the next business day.

5.3. After 2 hours, a store order cannot be changed or canceled. Use the return policy instead. See "Returns and Refunds".

5.4. Event tickets follow their own cancellation rules. See "Events and Tickets".
