# Pre-orders

## 1. How pre-orders work

1.1. Some vinyl records are sold as pre-orders before they are manufactured.

1.2. A pre-order vinyl record costs $32.

1.3. Payment is taken at checkout. With Afterpay, the first of 4 payments is made at checkout. See "Payments and Orders".

1.4. Each pre-order product page shows the expected shipping month. If the date changes, customers who pre-ordered receive an email.

## 2. Limit

2.1. Pre-order vinyl is limited to 2 copies per customer.

2.2. The 2-copy limit applies per customer, including separate orders.

2.3. If a customer orders more than 2 copies, the extra copies are canceled and refunded.

## 3. Orders with pre-order and in-stock items

3.1. If an order contains both a pre-order item and in-stock items, the whole order ships when the pre-order item is ready.

3.2. To get in-stock items sooner, place them in a separate order.

## 4. Returns

4.1. Pre-order vinyl is non-returnable, except if it arrives damaged or the wrong item was shipped. See "Returns and Refunds".
