# About 044 Records

## 1. Who we are

1.1. 044 Records is an independent record label based in Chicago, Illinois. It was founded in 2022 by a Ukrainian-American creative collective.

1.2. The name "044" is a personal reference to the founders' Ukrainian roots.

## 2. What we do

2.1. Release electronic music and alternative pop by artists based in the United States, on streaming platforms and on vinyl.

2.2. Run an online store with merchandise and vinyl records. See "Store and Catalog".

2.3. Organize live events and sell tickets on our website. See "Events and Tickets".

2.4. Accept demos from new artists through the website form. See "Demo Submissions".

## 3. Artist roster

3.1. Current 044 Records artists: SSSKYLER, fallencastle, goshaOPG, LALASH, MALA 044, Dima Kolbasenko, Olivar, BRUNO, finka, BELKA.

3.2. 044 Records does not share artists' personal contact details.

## 4. Customer support
