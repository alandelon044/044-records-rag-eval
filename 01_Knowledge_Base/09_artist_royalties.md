# Artist Royalties and Payments

## 1. Statements and payments

1.1. Statements and payments are issued quarterly, within 30 calendar days after each quarter ends.

1.2. Quarters are calendar quarters:

| Quarter | Period | Statement and payment by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | January–March | April 30 |
| Q2 | April–June | July 30 |
| Q3 | July–September | October 30 |
| Q4 | October–December | January 30 |

1.3. Statements are available in the artist portal on the 044 Records website.

## 2. Payment threshold

2.1. Payment is made when the artist balance is at least $100.

2.2. Smaller balances roll over to the next quarter.

2.3. Statements are issued every quarter, whatever the balance. The $100 threshold applies only to payments.

## 3. Payment methods

3.1. Artists are paid by U.S. bank transfer (ACH) or PayPal.

3.2. To change payment details, the artist updates them in the artist portal. Changes made after a quarter ends apply from the next payment.

## 4. Distribution

4.1. 044 Records distributes artists' releases to major streaming platforms.

4.2. Royalty shares are set in each artist's individual contract.
