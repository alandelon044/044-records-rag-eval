# Shipping

## 1. Where we ship

1.1. Shipping is available only within the contiguous United States.

1.2. The label does not ship to Alaska, Hawaii, or outside the United States.

## 2. Cost

2.1. Standard shipping is $6 per order.

2.2. Orders of $75 or more before tax ship free. The threshold is checked per order. Separate orders cannot be combined to reach it.

## 3. Delivery time

3.1. In-stock orders ship within 1–3 business days after the order is placed.

3.2. After the order ships, delivery takes 1–2 business days in Chicago and 3–7 business days elsewhere.

3.3. Pre-orders ship when the record is ready. See "Pre-orders".

## 4. Tracking

4.1. When an order ships, the customer receives an email with a tracking link.

4.2. If the tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact support@044records.example with your order number.
