# Frequently Asked Questions

**How much is shipping?**
Standard shipping is $6 per order. Orders of $75 or more before tax ship free.

**Do you ship to Alaska, Hawaii, or other countries?**
No. Shipping is available only within the contiguous United States.

**How long does delivery take?**
In-stock orders ship within 1–3 business days. After that, delivery takes 1–2 business days in Chicago and 3–7 business days elsewhere.

**Can I exchange a hoodie for a different size?**
Exchanges are not offered. To get a different size, return the item and place a new order.

**Can I return a vinyl record?**
In-stock vinyl records may be returned within 14 calendar days after delivery if the shrink wrap is unopened. Opened vinyl cannot be returned, except if it arrived damaged or the wrong item was shipped.

**Can I return a pre-order vinyl?**
Pre-order vinyl is non-returnable, except if it arrives damaged or the wrong item was shipped.

**My order arrived damaged. What should I do?**
Report damage or an incorrect item within 48 hours after delivery and include photos. The label offers a free replacement or a full refund. The label emails a free prepaid return label, so you do not pay to send the item back.

**How many copies of a pre-order vinyl can I buy?**
Pre-order vinyl is limited to 2 copies per customer, including separate orders.

**Can I get a refund for my concert ticket?**
Yes, if a cancellation request is made at least 7 calendar days before the event. Requests made less than 7 calendar days before the event are not refunded. Exceptions: if the label cancels an event, full refunds are automatic within 10 business days. If the label postpones an event, a ticket holder may request a full refund within 14 calendar days after notice, even if the event is less than 7 days away.

**Can I send my demo by email?**
No. Demos are accepted through the website form only, not by email.

**I sent a demo a month ago and heard nothing. What does that mean?**
Review takes up to 30 calendar days. No response by then means the demo was declined.

**When do artists get paid?**
Statements are issued every quarter, within 30 calendar days after each quarter ends, whatever the balance. Payment is made with the statement when the artist balance is at least $100. Smaller balances roll over to the next quarter.

**How do I contact support?**
Email support@044records.example. Support hours are Monday–Friday, 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Central Time. The team replies within 2 business days.
