# Events and Tickets

## 1. Buying tickets

1.1. Tickets for 044 Records events are sold only on our website.

1.2. Tickets are e-tickets. They arrive by email after checkout. Show the QR code at the door, on your phone or printed.

1.3. Ticket limits per customer, per event:

| Sale period | Limit per customer |
| --- | --- |
| Presale | up to 4 tickets |
| General sale | up to 8 tickets |
| High-demand show (any sale period) | up to 2 tickets |

1.4. Presale and general sale dates are shown on each event page. High-demand shows are marked "High demand" on the event page. For these shows, the 2-ticket limit applies in every sale period.

1.5. Ticket limits apply per customer, including separate orders. Tickets bought in presale count toward the general sale limit, so one customer can have up to 8 tickets per event in total for regular shows. For high-demand shows, the total limit is 2 tickets per event. Tickets above the limit are canceled and refunded.

1.6. Age policy: events are 21+ unless the event page says "All ages". Bring a valid photo ID.

## 2. Canceling your ticket

2.1. A customer receives a full refund, including service fees, if a cancellation request is made at least 7 calendar days before the event.

2.2. Requests made less than 7 calendar days before the event are not refunded.

2.3. To cancel, email support@044records.example with your order number.

## 3. If the label cancels or postpones an event

3.1. If the label cancels an event, full refunds are automatic within 10 business days.

3.2. If the label postpones an event, existing tickets are valid for the new date.

3.3. If it postpones an event, a ticket holder may request a full refund within 14 calendar days after notice.
