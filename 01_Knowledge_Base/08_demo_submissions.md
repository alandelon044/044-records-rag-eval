# Demo Submissions

## 1. How to submit

1.1. Demos are accepted through the website form only, not by email.

1.2. The form accepts up to 3 tracks per submission, as private streaming or download links.

1.3. We look for electronic music and alternative pop. We do not accept cover versions.

## 2. What happens next

2.1. Receipt is confirmed within 3 business days.

2.2. Review takes up to 30 calendar days.

2.3. No response by then means the demo was declined.

## 3. Submitting again

3.1. After a demo is declined, the artist may submit new material 90 calendar days after the original submission.
