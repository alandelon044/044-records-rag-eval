# Returns and Refunds

## 1. What can be returned

1.1. Regular merchandise (T-shirts and hoodies) may be returned within 14 calendar days after delivery if unused and with tags attached.

1.2. In-stock vinyl records may be returned within 14 calendar days after delivery if the shrink wrap is unopened. Opened vinyl cannot be returned, except if it arrived damaged or the wrong item was shipped.

1.3. Pre-order vinyl is non-returnable, except if it arrives damaged or the wrong item was shipped.

## 2. How returns work

2.1. To start a return, email support@044records.example with your order number.

2.2. For regular returns, the customer pays return shipping.

2.3. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the return is received.

2.4. If the customer paid the $6 shipping charge, it is refunded with any approved return, including a return of only part of the order.

## 3. Exchanges

3.1. Exchanges are not offered. To get a different size, return the item and place a new order.

## 4. Damaged or incorrect items

4.1. Report damage or an incorrect item within 48 hours after delivery and include photos.

4.2. The label offers a free replacement or a full refund.

4.3. The customer does not pay to send a damaged or incorrect item back. The label emails a free prepaid return label.

4.4. This exception overrides the pre-order vinyl rule and the opened vinyl rule.
