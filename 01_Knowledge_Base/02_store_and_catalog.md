# Store and Catalog

## 1. Products and prices

| Product | Price |
| --- | --- |
| T-shirt | $28 |
| Hoodie | $55 |
| Vinyl record | $32 |
| Pre-order vinyl record | $32 |

1.1. Prices exclude sales tax, which is calculated at checkout.

1.2. Prices do not include shipping. Standard shipping is $6. Orders of $75 or more before tax ship free. See "Shipping".

## 2. Pre-order limit

2.1. Pre-order vinyl is limited to 2 copies per customer. The limit applies per customer, including separate orders. See "Pre-orders".

## 3. Apparel sizes

3.1. T-shirts and hoodies come in sizes S, M, L, XL, and XXL. Both have a unisex fit.

| Size | Chest width, inches | Body length, inches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S | 18 | 28 |
| M | 20 | 29 |
| L | 22 | 30 |
| XL | 24 | 31 |
| XXL | 26 | 32 |

3.2. Chest width is measured flat, 1 inch below the armpit. Hoodies use the same chart.

3.3. Exchanges are not offered. To get a different size, return the item and place a new order. See "Returns and Refunds".

## 4. Stock

4.1. If an item is out of stock, the product page shows "Sold out". 044 Records does not take back-orders for sold-out merchandise.
